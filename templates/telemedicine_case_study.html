Telemedicine App Case Study

This case study highlights the development of a telemedicine app designed to connect patients with healthcare professionals remotely, providing appointment booking, medical records, and video consultations.

Project Overview

The telemedicine app was created to improve healthcare accessibility. It allows patients to book appointments, consult with doctors remotely, and access their medical records through an easy-to-use interface.

Key Features

  • Appointment booking
  • In-app chat and video calls
  • Patient and doctor profiles
  • Medical record management
  • AI-powered diagnosis suggestions

Technologies Used

  • Python
  • Flask
  • JavaScript
  • MySQL

Challenges & Solutions

Integrating secure video calls was challenging, but it was resolved by using WebRTC technology. Ensuring HIPAA-compliant data handling was another key focus.

Conclusion

The app provides a seamless experience for both doctors and patients, improving access to healthcare services, especially in remote areas.
