Opportunity Portal Case Study

This case study examines the development of an opportunity portal that connects job seekers with employers, tracks applications, and updates job statuses.

Project Overview

The Opportunity Portal aims to streamline the job application process for both job seekers and employers by offering a platform to manage applications, track job status, and communicate effectively.

Key Features

  • Job listings with search and filter options
  • Candidate profiles
  • Application tracking and status updates
  • Employer dashboards

Technologies Used

  • Python
  • Flask
  • MySQL
  • JavaScript

Challenges & Solutions

Building a scalable platform was a challenge, which was addressed by optimizing database queries and ensuring efficient handling of user requests.

Conclusion

The portal has successfully connected employers with qualified candidates, simplifying the recruitment process while also providing valuable metrics and insights to employers.
