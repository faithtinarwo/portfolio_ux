Case Study: Weather app

Problem Statement

People need reliable weather data quickly to plan their day, but most weather platforms are cluttered with ads, overly detailed data, or unnecessary complexity. Users require an app that offers the essentials in a simple, accessible, and aesthetically pleasing manner.

Objectives To create a user-centered weather app that provides key weather data (temperature, conditions, wind speed, etc.). To keep the interface simple and visually engaging, allowing users to quickly access and understand the information. To integrate a dark mode feature for better accessibility and user preference.

Solution

Users can view updated weather details (temperature, description, wind speed, etc.) for their chosen city. A customizable dark mode that enhances user comfort. Simple icons visually represent weather conditions (sunny, rainy, cloudy), making data more engaging.

Challenges & Solutions

Initially, network issues or incorrect city names led to errors or blank data displays. I implemented error handling to alert users with a clear message if data isn’t available. I faced challenges ensuring design consistency between light and dark modes. Testing on various devices helped fine-tune this feature.
